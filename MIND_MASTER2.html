How To Play

🧩 Objective: Reconstruct the shuffled picture before time runs out!


👆 Controls: Click/Tap tiles, use Arrow Keys, or Swipe to slide tiles into the empty space.


❤️ Lives: Trying to move an invalid tile costs 1 life. Lose 3 and it's Game Over!


💡 Hints: Highlights the best next move, but reduces your final score.


⭐ Scoring: Solve faster with fewer moves to earn 3 stars!
